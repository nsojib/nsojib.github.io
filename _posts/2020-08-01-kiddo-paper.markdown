**Problem:** Young children need engaging, interactive tools to practice handwriting.
**Approach:** Developed an educational robot that learns to draw and interacts with children in handwriting scenarios.
**Result:** Enhanced handwriting learning experience for primary school children through robot-assisted interaction.
