**Key Contributions:** Led hardware and software development of a 24 DOF humanoid platform. Implemented control for coordinated arm and hand motion. Designed user-facing interaction interface.

**Impact:** One of the first publicly demonstrated humanoid robots in Bangladesh. Showcased at national events with broad media coverage. Inspired student-led robotics initiatives across the country.
