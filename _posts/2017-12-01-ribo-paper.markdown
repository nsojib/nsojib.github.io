**Problem:** Limited access to humanoid robot platforms in resource-constrained settings.
**Approach:** Designed and built a social humanoid robot from scratch using low-cost components.
**Result:** One of the first publicly demonstrated humanoid robots in Bangladesh with broad media coverage.
