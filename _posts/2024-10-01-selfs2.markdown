Real-world robot learning breaks down when humans give imperfect demonstrations—yet perfect data is rarely available. We present <strong>BED</strong> (Behavior Cloning for Error Detection), a self-supervised framework that automatically identifies and filters out incorrect human demonstrations, enabling robots to learn safe policies from messy, real-world data. Validated in RoboSuite simulation and on a physical Sawyer robot arm.
